# No-slop writing self-check

Run this on your own output before returning it. Answer each line pass or fail. Fix every fail and run the list again.

## Did you do the right job

1. Edit request answered with a full edited draft, not commentary or a plan?
2. Detect request answered with named patterns, quoted lines, and fixes - no rewrite, no score, no guess about AI authorship?
3. Ship-check answered with a clear verdict and the blocking issues quoted?

## Truth

1. Nothing added that the user did not supply: no stats, sources, URLs, quotes, customers, dates, or personal experience.
2. Every claim carries the same confidence the evidence supports - no upgraded certainty, no fog of stacked hedges.
3. Limits, conditions, costs, risks, exceptions, and dependencies all survived the edit.
4. Unsupported claims that need the user's facts are listed under Flagged rather than quietly smoothed over.

## Voice

1. The writer's vocabulary, cadence, bluntness, humor, and digressions are still recognizable.
2. Strong human sentences were left alone instead of tidied for consistency.
3. Cutting was proportional to the actual slop - nothing compressed so hard it lost character.
4. If a voice profile was filled in, its rules were followed and its banned words are absent.
5. The writer would read this and recognize it as theirs.

## Patterns

1. Binary contrasts, throat-clearing, faux-insight setups, rhetorical setups, and generic scene-setting are gone.
2. Colon reveals, superficial `-ing` analysis, importance puffery, fake-strong verbs, and synonym cycling are fixed.
3. Interpretive metadiscourse is gone: no telling the reader what to notice or how much a point matters.
4. Weasel attribution is replaced with a named source or flagged for the user.
5. The fake-profound kicker was deleted, not rewritten into a better metaphor.
6. No summary-recap ending; the piece ends on a concrete point, takeaway, or next action.
7. No invented framework, pillar count, maturity model, or score.
8. No symmetry padding: item counts, section lengths, and bullet shapes follow the content.
9. Formatting is functional - no emoji headings, decorative bold, bullets that should be prose, or headings over tiny sections.
10. Em dashes: none in short copy, at most one or two in a long piece.
11. Banned words, empty adverbs, and empty phrases are gone unless quoted as examples.

## Substance

1. Every sentence passes the portability test or was made specific to this subject.
2. Specific facts and numbers were protected, never smoothed into generic importance.
3. Active voice with a human subject wherever the actor matters.
4. Each paragraph does one job; no stranded one-line paragraphs for drama.
5. Headings, if any, say what is under them.
6. The channel rules for this piece were applied, and only one channel's rules.

## Final read

1. Would the piece sound natural read aloud to a sharp colleague?
2. Edit: is the edited piece more useful than the original, not just smoother, and does the output include the full draft plus a short What changed list?
3. Ship-check: does the output stay a verdict, quoted blocking issues, and optional polish, with no rewrite of the draft?
