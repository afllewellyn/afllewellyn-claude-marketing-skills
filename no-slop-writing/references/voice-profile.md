# Voice profile

Fill this in and the skill edits toward your voice instead of a neutral one. Leave it as-is and the skill ignores it and infers voice from whatever draft you paste.

Keep it short. Ten honest lines beat three pages. Replace every `[bracket]` and delete any section that does not apply.

> Tip: keep a personal copy at `references/voice-profile.local.md` and add that filename to your `.gitignore` if you fork this repo publicly. The skill reads `voice-profile.local.md` first when it exists.
