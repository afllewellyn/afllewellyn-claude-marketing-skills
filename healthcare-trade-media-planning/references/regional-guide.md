# Regional expansion guide


### United States

Default strongest publication ecosystems:

- Dental: EndeavorB2B Dental Group, Dentistry Today, Conexiant/ADA, AGD, Decisions in Dentistry.
- HIS: HIMSS Media, Becker’s, Healthcare Innovation, Modern Healthcare, HFMA, HBMA.
- Medical Surgical: AORN, ASA Monitor, Anesthesiology News, AANA, HPN, APIC, Infection Control Today, WoundSource/HMP.

### Canada

Starter targets:

- ORNAC Journal for perioperative nursing.
- Canadian healthcare association publications should be researched per product: perioperative, infection prevention, dental association, health IT, and hospital finance.
- For small-budget tests, ORNAC-style web/e-blast/social packages are likely more realistic than large U.S. national minimums.

### UK / Europe

Starter targets:

- Dental: BDJ / BDJ In Practice / BDA portfolio, The Probe, Dental Tribune International Europe.
- HIS: HIMSS Europe, Healthcare IT News global/HIMSS network, HealthManagement.org, Digital Health UK, national NHS/health IT publications.
- Medical Surgical: specialty association publications by audience: perioperative nursing, anesthesia, infection prevention, wound care, surgery.
- Biopharma: European Pharmaceutical Manufacturer (EPM, BPA-audited, EMEA anchor), BioProcess International Europe, BPI Europe event.

### APAC / Global

Starter targets:

- Dental Tribune International regional editions.
- HIMSS APAC / Healthcare IT News global channels.
- Specialty conference media around dental, anesthesia, surgery, infection prevention, and wound care.
- Biopharma: BioProcess International (global; BPI Asia / Kyoto event), GEN, Fierce Biotech global newsletters.
- Always localize by country because licensing, audience files, and association control vary heavily.
