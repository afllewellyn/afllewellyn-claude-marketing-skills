## Strong initial recommendations by product

### Restorative dental products

_Example category — replace with `[YOUR RESTORATIVE PRODUCT]`._

**Start with:** Dental Economics, Dentistry Today, Conexiant/Inside Dentistry, AGD, Dental Tribune International.  
**Best channels:** Sponsored clinical case, newsletter sponsorship, web display retargeting, CE/webinar where claims support it.  
**Avoid first:** broad hospital media, medical surgical journals, general consumer dental sites.

### Preventive / dental hygiene products

_Example category — replace with `[YOUR PREVENTIVE/HYGIENE PRODUCT]`._

**Start with:** RDH, DentistryIQ, AGD, ADA/Conexiant, Dental Tribune International.  
**Best channels:** Hygiene newsletter, prevention education, CE, practice workflow content.

### Temperature management devices

_Example category — replace with `[YOUR TEMPERATURE MANAGEMENT PRODUCT]`._

**Start with:** ASA Monitor, Anesthesiology News, AANA, AORN, ORNAC for Canada.  
**Best channels:** Anesthesia newsletter, perioperative newsletter, sponsored education, web display around anesthesia/perioperative content. Add PLN for OR-leadership lead gen and Beyond the Mask for a fast CRNA podcast layer.  
**Sequencing note:** anesthesia/CRNA-first; bring OR-leadership placements only when lead gen is the goal.

### Preoperative decolonization antiseptics

_Example category — replace with `[YOUR ANTISEPTIC/DECOLONIZATION PRODUCT]`._

**Start with:** AACN (anchor — ICU/critical care), APIC (infection prevention); AORN or PLN as secondary OR-leadership extension; Periop Roundtable Webinar for lead gen.  
**Best channels:** Consider leading with email if it outperforms other formats for your brand — critical-care newsletter + IP newsletter, plus web display and a sponsored protocol/webinar layer.  
**Avoid first:** AORN-first framing (this is a critical-care/IP product, not an OR-field product), broad hospital media.

### Surgical field antisepsis products

_Example category — replace with `[YOUR SURGICAL ANTISEPSIS PRODUCT]`._

**Start with:** AORN (OR nursing/sterile field), APIC, Infection Control Today, Healthcare Purchasing News, ORNAC Canada.  
**Best channels:** Perioperative newsletter, infection prevention newsletter, sponsored protocol article, webinar/CNE. Add PLN for OR-leadership lead gen.

### NPWT devices / wound dressings

_Example category — replace with `[YOUR WOUND CARE PRODUCT]`._

**Start with:** WoundSource/HMP, Wound Management & Prevention, Today’s Wound Clinic, APIC, Healthcare Purchasing News.  
**Best channels:** wound care newsletter, sponsored clinical education, webinar, condition/procedure-specific content hub.

### Health Information Systems / RCM / coding / CDI

**Start with:** HIMSS Media, Becker’s, HFMA, HBMA, Healthcare Innovation, Modern Healthcare.  
**Best channels:** lead-gen webinar, sponsored thought leadership, health IT newsletter, executive roundtable, revenue cycle issue alignment.

### Biopharma purification / filtration / bioprocessing

**Start with:** BioProcess International (anchor), EPM for EMEA, Fierce Biotech for reach, BioPharma Dive for exec brand, BioPharm International for additional lead-gen formats. Next RFP: GEN, Bioprocess Online.  
**Best channels:** Gated technical content (whitepapers, webcasts) and content syndication on BPI/EPM for lead gen; broad newsletter/display reach on Fierce; studioID custom content on BioPharma Dive; BioPharm International's format menu includes display banners, dedicated email blast, custom email, Dedicated Dialogue, sponsored e-book, and a content engagement hub — useful additional lead-gen formats to consider.  
**Avoid first:** treating Fierce Biotech as the topical anchor (it is reach, not bioprocessing); third-party rate estimates in client docs.
