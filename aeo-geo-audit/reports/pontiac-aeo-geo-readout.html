State of play

When a buyer types the name, all four engines find Pontiac, describe the 20% self-serve margin, the no-minimum model and the CTV-first bidder, and Google's AI Overview does the same from Reddit, YouTube and drive.pontiac.media. When a buyer asks the question that comes before knowing the name, Pontiac is absent and the engines name StackAdapt, The Trade Desk, AdLib, Vibe, Roku and Simpli.fi instead, sourced from those competitors' own comparison articles, Reddit threads and G2.

Two things make this fixable rather than structural. The gates are open: neither site blocks any AI crawler, both are server-rendered so crawlers see full copy without JavaScript, and G2 sentiment is strong (4.8 from 16 reviews, support scored 10.0). And Pontiac's real offer, no minimum, flat margin, Netflix and Disney CTV access, log-level data, an MCP server for ChatGPT and Claude, fits the winning queries better than most of the brands being cited. What is missing is content on Pontiac's own domain that answers those queries, and basic crawl hygiene on the wiki where the most citable material already lives.

One cost of the gap is already visible: with no pricing page on pontiac.media, Perplexity tells buyers Pontiac costs "$25/month with a free version" (from SourceForge) and Claude reports "a $99 fee and $1,000 minimum" (from Clutch). The correct figures sit on a wiki page no engine has cited.

Five priorities, ranked by impact against effort

  1. Publish the comparison and category pages the engines actually cite

    Four pages on pontiac.media: Pontiac vs StackAdapt, Pontiac vs Simpli.fi, Best self-serve DSPs with no minimum spend (2026), Trade Desk alternatives for agencies under $10k/month. Answer-first opening, a comparison table with real fees and minimums, a named author. Add a fifth page on the Pontiac MCP server for ChatGPT and Claude Code and list it in the MCP directories Perplexity and Claude cited.

    Why: unbranded discovery share is 3 of 28. The two comparison prompts Pontiac lost outright are its own positioning. Every engine cited competitor listicles (AdLib, Epom, Vibe, StackAdapt) for those answers, and Pontiac's MCP docs were cited 0 times in 8 MCP observations.

    Effort: mediumOwner: marketing lead, founders for the fee and positioning claims

  2. Put the real pricing on pontiac.media and correct the third-party listings

    One pricing page ($100 one-time entry, 20% self-serve margin, 30% managed, no minimum). Then update G2's pricing entry (dated 2022), SourceForge and Slashdot ("$25/month, free version") and Clutch ("$1,000 minimum").

    Why: Perplexity is telling buyers Pontiac costs $25/month. Claude says $99 and a $1,000 minimum. ChatGPT had to read the Terms & Conditions to find the 20%. The correct numbers sit on a wiki page no engine has cited.

    Effort: lowOwner: marketing lead, one afternoon

  3. Make the wiki citable

    Add robots.txt and an XML sitemap, promote doc titles to H1, generate meta descriptions, fix 47 duplicate titles, turn the 89,000-word hub pages into indexes with a two-line summary per child, expose last-updated dates, add Organization and TechArticle schema.

    Why: 700+ pages of the most specific documentation in the category rank for zero keywords, have 3 referring domains, and were cited in 0 of 80 answers. A 90,000-word page cannot be chunked and attributed by an AI engine.

    Effort: mediumOwner: web developer, about a week of template work

  4. Fix the marketing site's crawl signals

    Replace the stale eight-URL sitemap.txt with the WordPress XML sitemap. Fix the Organization schema that points at http://172.31.40.15/. 301 the four legacy .html pages. Promote blog post titles to H1. Server-render the contact form.

    Why: the schema bug weakens entity disambiguation for a brand that shares its name with a car company (319 logged AI answers for "Pontiac Intelligence" are about Firebirds). An AI agent asked to request a demo cannot find a form in the HTML.

    Effort: lowOwner: web developer, one day

  5. Show up where the engines are reading, and keep the baseline running

    Founders answer the r/programmatic threads the engines cite ("self-serve DSP recs", "buy CTV without minimum", "fully transparent DSPs"). Post the 2025 to 2026 news (Peer39 and InterMedia show-level CPA, MCP launch, Beet.TV) on pontiac.media. Re-run the 20-prompt baseline monthly and pull the AI-bot server logs once to confirm crawlers are actually getting 200s.

    Why: Reddit was the most-cited domain across all 80 answers and already recommends Pontiac organically in one thread. The blog's freshness signal is 14 months stale. Visibility is volatile, so the number only means something as a trend.

    Effort: low to medium, ongoingOwner: CEO or co-founder for Reddit voice, marketing for cadence, hosting admin for logs
